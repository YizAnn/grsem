# grsem 0.2.0.9000

- Core grouped/sparse-group SEM paths, relative rho settings, and explicit
  convergence diagnostics.
- Structural regression export with selected_edges() and DAG legality checks
  with validate_dag(); neither is causal discovery.
- Gaussian held-out loss alignment and fixed-schema failure records for CV
  and model comparisons.
- ML-only post-selection refit, synthetic executable examples, and parameter,
  optimizer, graph, and evaluation regression tests.
- Repository-local script/checkpoint outputs and separate build staging.
- Bayesian, binary DWLS, and PC/FCI/GES interfaces remain Experimental.
  NOTEARS and prior_predict remain Not Implemented.
